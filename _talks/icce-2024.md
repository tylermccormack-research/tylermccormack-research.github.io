This presentation gives a brief overview of the methods and results from _Remote sensing of wave-orbital velocities in the surfzone_ (McCormack et. al., 2025), highlighting how we can use surface velocities measured with cameras and PIV to estimate nearbed velocities and velocity power spectral density.
