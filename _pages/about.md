As of September 2026, I am in Grenoble, France exploring coastal applications for the Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) satellite mission and developing algorithms to optimize SWOT data to study nearshore processes. Specifically, I am quantifying how SWOT measurements are affected by various wave conditions and using the SWOT pixel cloud data to estimate wave setup.
